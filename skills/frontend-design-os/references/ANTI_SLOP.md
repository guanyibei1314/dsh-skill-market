# Anti-AI-Slop Gate

Fail the design unless explicit product/reference evidence justifies the pattern.

## Decorative defaults to reject
- purple/blue “AI” gradients used as a substitute for hierarchy;
- glow blobs and neon haze behind ordinary SaaS content;
- a card for every metric, feature, status, and explanation;
- large centered marketing hero inside an operational dashboard;
- decorative pills/badges that encode no state;
- fake metrics, fake social proof, fake logos, fabricated user counts;
- generic three-column feature grids used only to fill space;
- arbitrary rounded rectangles nested inside rounded rectangles;
- excessive 999px pills;
- repeated shadows as the primary separation mechanism;
- meaningless icon rows;
- headings such as “Transform your workflow” when the product can state a concrete job instead.

## Structural defaults to reject
- desktop tables squeezed into unreadable mobile widths;
- giant `h-screen` sections that clip on small laptops;
- navigation with too many equal-priority actions;
- mobile layouts created solely by hiding desktop columns;
- content hierarchy expressed only through color;
- invented sections added after the design contract is locked.

## Allowed only with evidence
Gradients, glass, glow, bento grids, cards, pills, large centered heroes, dense dashboards, and expressive motion are not banned. They require clear support from the product type or accepted reference/design direction.
